# Requirement change after the manual sleep run (2026-10-01)

Finding F (no chime after a real sleep) was investigated with throwaway diagnostics. The first attempt, replacing an audio context whose clock stalled across the sleep, was reverted. The second diagnostic showed the page doing everything right: the replacement context was `running` and its clock advanced normally after the chime was scheduled, yet nothing was audible on the owner's laptop right after the wake (the audio device was not ready). The page cannot see that.

**Owner decision:** a User who comes back needs no late sound. A completion noticed **more than 2 minutes** after its true moment (a *stale* completion) plays no chime and shows no sound-unavailable notice. It is still credited, and the break still waits at full length. The 2 minutes is a proxy, because the page cannot tell a sleep from a freeze, and it keeps the chime of a hidden tab throttled by up to about a minute.

| # | Resolution |
|---|---|
| F | Closed by the requirement change, not as a defect. The 5 s On-time rule for auto-start is unchanged; a completion 5 s to 2 min late still leaves the break waiting and chimes once; later than 2 min it is silent |

**Changed:** `src/logic/controls.js` (`STALE_COMPLETION_MS`, `isStaleCompletion`), `src/ui/index.js` (`applyCompletionCue` takes `now`; `renderCycle` passes it). **Specs:** `sensory-feedback/spec.md` (decision note, AC-06b, §3 non-goals, AC-10 midnight wording), `sensory-feedback/sad.md`, `sensory-feedback/ux-flows.md`, `break-flow/spec.md` (AC-03, §6 row, test-plan rows, edge cases), `break-flow/sad.md`, `break-flow/ux-flows.md`. **Not edited, as history:** the task breakdown files, the sensory-feedback CHANGELOG and PR body, and ADR-0001's AC-06b line. **Tests:** the late-chime tests were rewritten around the 2-minute line (a 2-minute return still chimes, a 2-minute-and-1-ms return is silent), including the midnight-credit e2e.
